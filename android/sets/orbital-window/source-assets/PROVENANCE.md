JPEG-encoded source files use `.jpg` Android resource names without changing bytes. The preview is the genuine reduced PNG from the immutable snapshot `previews/` directory. Owner approval: `android/sets/orbital-window/approval.md`.

Clock source revision 2 adds the accepted Pencil V1 frames `dnYIk`, `m4SBgo`, `sagao` as preview-only PNGs and native S/M/L RemoteViews layouts. The bundled variable Manrope TTF is the unmodified official Google Fonts file from `google/fonts/ofl/manrope` (retrieved 2026-09-24, SIL Open Font License 1.1 in `android/sets/orbital-window/MANROPE_OFL.txt`). The live face uses system `AnalogClock` with authored dial/hour/minute drawables; preview PNGs do not replace its system-driven hands.
